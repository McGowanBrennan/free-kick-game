One flick. Five kicks. No mercy.
How to play
Shoot Swipe from the ball to where you want it to go and lift your finger there. Swipe faster for more power: the trail turns from blue to red. Too hard and it rises over.
Curl Bend your swipe: the ball sets off the way you start and curls round to where you finish. Start outside the wall, finish at the far post.
Chip Swipe up and snap straight back down: the top of your swipe is the aim. From the penalty spot, softly down the middle, it's a Panenka.
Keep Flick from the keeper toward where you want to dive; the ring shows where your hands will go. Straight up jumps. Tap to stay big in the middle. While the shooter lines up, tap a taunt to get in their head.
Field goals Swipe over the bar between the uprights: your swipe calls the snap. Lift it over the hands at the line, aim (or bend it) into the wind, and mind the cable cam swinging over the field: it'll have moved by the time the ball gets there.
